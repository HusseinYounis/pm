🗻 The Story — A Trip on Road 60

You are driving from Tulkarem to Jenin — two cities in the West Bank, roughly 60 km apart. Before leaving home, you planned the trip carefully: you estimated that fuel, food, and checkpoint tolls would cost ₪10 per km, giving a total trip budget of ₪600. Think of yourself as the project manager of this journey — you set the plan, track spending, and decide what to do next.

By midday, your plan said you should have covered 24 km. But checkpoints and slow traffic on Road 60 delayed you — you have only driven 20 km so far. You pull over at a petrol station, check your wallet, and count everything spent: fuel fills, a snack at a roadside stand, and a small parking fee at a checkpoint — a total of ₪180 spent so far.

Step 1 of 5 — The Four Core Values (القيم الأساسية الأربع)

PV Planned Value  (القيمة المخططة)

🚗 In the story: Your plan said reach 24 km by midday. How much should that 24 km have cost according to your budget?
PV = the budgeted cost of the work you planned to finish by this point in time. It answers: “How much did the plan say we should have spent on the planned work?”
PV = Planned km × Budget Rate
PV = km × ₪/km = ₪

EV Earned Value  (القيمة المكتسبة)

🚗 In the story: You actually drove only 20 km — not 24. EV values that actual progress in budget terms: what should those 20 km have cost?
EV = the budgeted cost of work actually completed. It answers: “How much should the work we actually finished have cost us (at the planned rate)?”
EV = Actual km Done × Budget Rate
EV = km × ₪/km = ₪

Step 2 of 5 — Variances: On Time? On Budget? (الانحرافات)

Variances measure the gap in money terms. Negative = bad (behind / over) · Positive = good (ahead / under).

SV Schedule Variance  (انحراف الجدول الزمني)

🚗 In the story: You planned ₪240 worth of progress (24 km) but only achieved ₪200 (20 km). The gap of ₪40 is the schedule variance — you are ₪40 behind your planned progress.
SV = how far ahead or behind schedule you are, expressed as money. Negative SV → behind schedule. Positive SV → ahead of schedule.
SV = EV − PV
SV = EV − PV = ₪ − ₪ = ₪

Result is negative / positive (circle) → I am behind / ahead of schedule (circle). In trip terms: I am km behind plan.

🚗 In the story: For the 20 km driven, the budget says you should have spent ₪200. But you only spent ₪180. The ₪20 difference is the cost variance — you spent less than planned for what you completed.
CV = how much over or under budget you are for the completed work. Negative CV → over budget. Positive CV → under budget.
CV = EV − AC
CV = EV − AC = ₪ − ₪ = ₪

Result is negative / positive (circle) → I am over / under budget (circle). I saved ₪ on the 20 km driven.

Step 3 of 5 — Performance Indices: How Efficient? (مؤشرات الأداء)

Indices express efficiency as a ratio — no money units. They allow comparing projects of any size. = 1.0 on plan · > 1.0 better than planned · < 1.0 worse than planned.

SPI = work completed per unit of work planned. SPI = 0.83 means you are achieving 83% of the planned schedule pace.
