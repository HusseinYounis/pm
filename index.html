Welcome to the Course

This course introduces engineering students to the principles, tools, and practices of professional project management. Whether you are building a bridge, developing software, or managing a manufacturing plant upgrade, the ability to plan, execute, and control projects is one of the most valuable skills an engineer can possess.

Note🎯 Course Philosophy

This course is built on a simple idea: Project Management is an engineering discipline. It requires systematic thinking, quantitative analysis, and structured problem solving — the same skills you use in your technical courses, applied to the management of complex, real-world endeavours.

Course Structure

What You Will Learn

0.0.1 Part I — Foundations

  • ✅ Understand what projects are and why they fail
  • ✅ Analyse projects using the systems view and three-sphere model
  • ✅ Navigate the five process groups and ten knowledge areas
  • ✅ Understand organisational structures, culture, and their impact on PM

0.0.2 Part II — Planning

  • ✅ Build Work Breakdown Structures (WBS)
  • ✅ Schedule projects using Gantt charts and CPM
  • ✅ Allocate and level resources efficiently
  • ✅ Develop realistic project budgets

0.0.3 Part III — Execution & Control

  • ✅ Identify, analyse, and respond to risks
  • ✅ Monitor costs using Earned Value Management
  • ✅ Implement quality assurance and control processes
  • ✅ Manage change and handle project crises

0.0.4 Part IV — Modern Approaches

  • ✅ Apply Agile and Scrum to engineering projects
  • ✅ Lead high-performance project teams
  • ✅ Close projects professionally and capture lessons learned
  • ✅ Build a personal PM toolkit for your career
